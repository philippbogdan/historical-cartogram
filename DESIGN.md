# Human space

## Scene and register

A curious person watches an atlas on a laptop in ordinary daylight. A pale, cool paper surface keeps fine boundaries and dense population marks legible. The experience is a single interactive scientific illustration, not a dashboard.

## Visual language

Physical reference: a printed world atlas spread, with engraved cell boundaries brought into motion. Full-palette colour is reserved for the cartography. The page uses cool paper and deep blue ink; no cards, gradients in text or decorative glass.

## Typography

Chivo, locally hosted under the SIL Open Font License. Solid, open map lettering and a strong but compact headline. One family with clear weight and scale contrast. Reflex choices considered and rejected: Inter, Space Grotesk, Instrument Serif. Body and controls remain compact and readable, with larger type reserved for the reading rule.

## Colour

Paper: oklch(97.7% 0.007 230). Ink: oklch(26% 0.035 250). Secondary ink: oklch(47% 0.025 250). Accent: oklch(53% 0.17 255). Geographic regions use consistent green, blue, rose, orange and violet families. Quantitative differences use a labelled blue-to-neutral-to-red diverging scale; missing data is explicitly neutral and distinct from the midpoint.

## Composition

Small identity at the top, one strong reading rule, then a large uninterrupted map. The reversible scrubber sits immediately below the map. Place labels and a persistent selection keep the viewer oriented. Further explanation opens inline below the main experience.

## Motion and interaction

The transformation is controlled by a continuous slider and a play/pause button. The same population units, country shapes and selected location move together. Playback pauses at endpoints and reverses. Manual scrubbing pauses playback. No entrance theatrics. Respect reduced motion and pause when the tab is hidden.

Expansion builds smoothly and has a long exponential settling phase. A spatially averaged
inverse-square repulsion model drives the collective timing along the atlas deformation.
Collective momentum carries through direction changes, with a damped turn. Pause and direct
scrubbing stop the motion. There is no random per-particle jitter or decorative bounce.

## Responsive behaviour

Keep the map visible on phones, keep controls touch-sized, and stack explanation rather than shrinking it into a sidebar. Zoom permits inspection of individual population units. Do not rely on hover for essential information.
