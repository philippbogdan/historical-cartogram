# The paper: what to claim (as of 2026-09-03)

Working title: The optimal-transport cartogram: least-movement density equalisation at 100 m.

Claims that survive the prior-art check (PRIOR_WORK.md):
1. A population cartogram as the Brenier map: the unique gradient-of-a-convex-potential map that equalises
   density, hence zero rotation everywhere and least total squared displacement. Nobody has published one;
   the transport-map cartogram in the literature is flow-based (Gastner, Seguy and More 2018).
2. A spectral fixed-point Monge-Ampere solver on a laptop GPU: state = FFT of the right-hand side, Hessian
   from bounded multipliers, float32 exact, 4096 in about a minute, 8192 in float64 in forty minutes;
   continuation in the humanity share to reach the pure limit; the ocean-only buffer that keeps land exact.
3. Metrics that separate methods: population-weighted density error, anisotropy, twist (zero for OT, not for
   diffusion), displacement, recognisability (Procrustes), seams; OT beats diffusion on displacement,
   anisotropy and twist at equal accuracy.
4. Nested solves: a 10 km global map composed with 100 m window solves corrected in the output space, so a
   city's blocks get their own areas (Delhi: spread -1.15/+2.90 to -0.10/+0.21 at 300 m). Carroll and Moore
   2008 nested by user focus with diffusion; nesting by data resolution with OT is new.
5. The lens grammar: painting a measure on another measure's cartogram is the Radon-Nikodym derivative; GDP
   per person-year as the worked example; the humeter as the unit.
6. Rendering at 100 m through the continuous warp (vector boundaries warped vertex by vertex, textures by the
   inverse map), a 126-epoch timeline through the same solver, and the semi-discrete power diagram of
   humanity (one cell per million people) as the discrete counterpart.

Not claimed: sphere-native OT (Weller et al. 2016 did Monge-Ampere on the sphere for meshes); any priority on
area-preserving OT flattening (Zhao et al. 2013).

Venues: Cartography and Geographic Information Science (methods + pictures), IEEE VIS short paper (the
solver and metrics), or IJGIS. The humeter geometry (curvature, geodesics) is an essay, not a paper.
