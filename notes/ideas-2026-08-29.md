# Plan review: what I would add or do differently (2026-08-29)

Candidate nodes, not yet in PLAN.md. Phil picks; IDs continue the existing series.

## Do differently

- **Time interpolation by measure, not by displacement (T3).** Between two epochs, warp the blended
  measure (1-s) mu_t1 + s mu_t2, exactly like the humanity share. Every intermediate frame is then a
  true cartogram of a stated measure; interpolating displacement fields is a tween of nothing.
- **Solver resolution vs texture resolution (S3).** The warp is smooth at 30 km; solving at 8192 is the
  ceiling worth having. "Operate at full resolution" means pushing the 100 m texture through the 8192
  warp, which is a rendering task, not a solver task. Nothing is lost.
- **A gallery page instead of eyeballing files (X6).** Generate `experiments/gallery.html` from INDEX
  with thumbnails and metrics; the F2-F4 decisions get made on one page.
- **Pure means M3.** The Poisson iteration cannot deliver share 1; stop pushing it and build the
  back-and-forth solver. Semi-discrete OT (below) is the other fold-free route.

## Methods

- **M11 semi-discrete OT (Laguerre cells).** One site per N people (say 100k) gets a convex polygon of
  exactly equal area: no folds by construction, exact areas, and a new picture (a power diagram of
  humanity). Also the natural engine for "one person per pixel" (L7). Code exists (Merigot, Levy).
- **M12 OT on the sphere natively**, only if the equal-area cylinder shows polar damage (was A3).

## Metrics and analysis renders

- **X7 recognisability, quantified.** Per country: shape distance between warped and original outline
  after the best similarity transform (Gastner's shape error); and label legibility, the share of
  countries whose warped area can hold their name.
- **X9 twist map.** Local rotation angle from the polar decomposition of the Jacobian: OT should be
  zero everywhere, diffusion is not. A metric and a picture.
- **R7 equipotentials.** Level sets of the OT potential phi drawn on the map: the contour lines of
  population gravity. Meaningful, and beautiful.
- **R8 flow lines.** Arrows or streamlines from geography to cartogram: where the space went.
- **R10 ghost coastline.** The original outline faintly under the warped one.

## Data and layers

- **D6 GHS-POP 1975-2030 (5-year, 1 km / 100 m).** The modern era at high resolution; HYDE only for
  10,000 BC to 1970. Needs one normalisation (HYDE totals to GHS totals) at the handover.
- **D7 GHS-BUILT and GHS-SMOD.** Built-up surface and degree of urbanisation: "urban vs rural" as a lens
  and as a mask for the 100 m texture.
- **A14 city labels.** Natural Earth populated places or GHS-UCDB urban centres (free, with population):
  the ~300 largest cities named at warped positions. Countries alone are not enough on a globe.
- **A15 rivers.** Natural Earth rivers through the warp: the Nile, Ganges, Yangtze as lines. The
  civilisation story is river-shaped.
- **T5 the future.** SSP gridded population projections (Jones and O'Neill 2016, 1/8 degree, 2010-2100):
  the timeline continues to 2100 under scenarios. Playback that does not stop at today.
- **T7 events on the map.** Reba's historical urban population points (3700 BC onwards) drawn and sized
  through each epoch's warp: cities appear, grow, vanish on the cartogram.
- **T6 peak time lens.** Per pixel, the epoch of maximum density: where the world already peaked.

## Lenses (extending L)

- **L9 the ratio family.** People / lights (wealth), people / cropland (who feeds whom), people / CO2,
  people / roads; each is one dnu/dmu picture and one sentence.
- **L10 age lens.** WorldPop age-sex grids: median age per pixel; the old world and the young world;
  under-15s as the next generation's cartogram.
- **L11 attention lens.** Wikipedia geotagged article density (free dump) or GDELT: where the world looks
  versus where it lives.
- **L12 the non-human world.** Earth by trees (biomass grids), by cropland, by protected land: the
  complement of the humeter, and the honest frame for "empty feels vast".
- **L13 cumulative person-years by epoch** (L3 as a slider): where human life has been lived up to year t.

## Viewer

- **V0 dev viewer now.** A local tile server over the GeoTIFFs and warped outputs (Leaflet, windowed
  reads): the only way to actually see 100 m globally before Phase 9. Built 2026-08-29.
- **V12 permalinks** for any (lens, time, method, share, view) state.
- **V13 compare mode**: swipe or side-by-side of two lenses or two methods.
- **V14 story mode**: a guided tour (Nile, Ganges, Java, the Atlantic seam).
- **V15 print export**: any view as PNG/SVG at 8k.

## Process

- Golden regression: the world at 512 with fixed knobs, metrics within tolerance, run before commits.
- Honesty labels on the artefact per layer: observed / modelled / projected, with the source.
- Order I would follow now: M3 -> A14 + A15 + R7 -> D2 full 100 m + V0 -> gallery (F2-F4) -> Phase 5.
